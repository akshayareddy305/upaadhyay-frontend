Our mission

Hiring teachers should be fast, fair and trustworthy.

Upaadhyay brings faculty and institutions onto one verified platform. Faculty build a single profile with their qualifications, experience and publications, and get discovered by institutions across India.

Institutions post unlimited openings, search verified profiles, shortlist candidates and invite them directly — without the noise of unverified listings.
