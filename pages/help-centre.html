Getting started

How do I register as faculty?

Click Register, choose Faculty, and complete your basic details. You can finish your academic profile afterwards.

Is Upaadhyay free?

Registration is free for faculty. Employers get an introductory free period.

Can I apply without a complete profile?

A complete profile improves visibility, but you can start with the basics.

For faculty

How do I apply for a job?

Open any job from Browse jobs and use the apply button. You must be logged in as faculty.

How do I update my profile?

Log in, open the profile menu and choose Update profile.

Who can see my profile?

Verified institutions can discover your profile to invite or shortlist you.

For employers

How do I post a job?

Log in as an employer, open your dashboard and use Post a job.

How do I search for faculty?

Use Browse candidates from your employer dashboard and filter by expertise.

How are institutions verified?

Institution details are reviewed before your profile and openings are published.

Account & login

I forgot my password

Use the forgot-password option on the login page. If it still fails, contact us.

How do I log out?

Use the Log out button in the top-right of the navigation bar.
